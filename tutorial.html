Step-by-Step Setup Instructions

1

Install and Open the CartForge App

In your Shopify Admin, navigate to Apps and select CartForge: Checkout Upsell. You will land on the Polaris Merchant Dashboard where you can view analytics and campaign status.

2

Configure Your Milestone Tiers & Order Bump

In the dashboard:

  • Set Target Threshold Amount (for example: $50 for Free Shipping or $100 for a Gift).
  • Select the reward type and choose a product from your catalog for the reward tier.
  • Enable the 1-Click Order Bump and choose your add-on product (e.g. Priority Handling).
  • Click Save Settings.
3

Add the Block to Checkout

Navigate to Settings > Checkout in your Shopify Admin. Click Customize on your active checkout profile. In the left panel, click + Add app block, select CartForge Checkout Bar, and position it directly into your order summary or above the shipping section. Click Save.

4

Verify on Storefront Checkout

Visit your storefront, add a product to the cart, and proceed to checkout. You will see the dynamic progress bar calculating the remaining amount to reach your reward tier. Adding additional items dynamically fills the bar and unlocks the milestone!
